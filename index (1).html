    🌟 Why "Zharab", the Swan & The Shield?

    • Name (ذَرَب): In Arabic, it denotes sharpness, eloquence, and intellectual precision—reflecting the tool's acute detection capabilities.

    • The Shield & Crystal Swan: The Shield represents impenetrable local defense, while the graceful Crystal Swan symbolizes silent, elegant vigilance beneath the surface waters (protecting system processes without disrupting them).

    • Privilege Abuse Trusted accounts and tools used beyond their purpose.
    • Process Injection Foreign code written into a legitimate process.
